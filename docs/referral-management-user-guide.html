Referral Management

A short guide to matching buyer invoices to referral leads, recording referrer fees, and collecting the referrer’s details.

Keep the two people clear:
The lead / buyer is the potential customer on the invoice. The referrer is the person who introduced that buyer and may earn a referral fee.

02 · Find a buyer invoice

Scan all referral leads

On the Referral Management page, tap Scan referral invoices. The scan checks every referral lead for an existing link and possible invoice matches.

1

Green: already linked

Linked Invoice = INV-… means this referral already has a linked invoice. Tap the invoice label to inspect it.

2

Amber: possible match

Same contact number invoice found or Same name invoice found is a suggestion based on the lead and invoice customer details. It has not been assigned yet.

3

Check before linking

The result also shows payment context, such as the amount received or paid percentage. Tap the invoice number / eye icon to inspect the invoice and payment record.

◉ Same contact number invoice found: INV-100123

A name or phone match can be another person. Treat amber results as candidates, not proof.

If there is no match, the card says “No matching invoice found.”

03 · Confirm the attribution

“Use for referral fee”

This admin button turns a verified amber candidate into the invoice attributed to this referral lead.

Same contact number invoice found: INV-100123
↗ Use for referral fee
1

Verify the invoice first

Open the quick view and confirm the invoice belongs to the referred buyer.

2

Review the confirmation

The app warns that this replaces another referral attribution on that invoice and may move this lead’s existing invoice link.

3

Confirm only when correct

The invoice is attributed to this referral. It appears on Referrer Fee Follow-up after the buyer has made a payment.

The buyer on the invoice does not change. This changes referral attribution only. If the match is uncertain, cancel and verify with the sales team.

06 · Request and complete details

WhatsApp + Referrer Info

Use the buttons on a referrer card to ask for missing details and share the update form.

1

WhatsApp with form link

Opens a WhatsApp chat with a prepared message and the referrer’s update link. Check the recipient and send the message.

2

Referrer Info

On the lead card, opens the referrer detail panel and its form link. On a follow-up card, opens the update form. Copy update link shares it through another channel.

3

Referrer completes and submits

The form updates the referrer profile and the referral lead details. The referrer can correct pre-filled information too.

Send the update link to the intended referrer. Ask them to review the identity and payment details before submitting.
